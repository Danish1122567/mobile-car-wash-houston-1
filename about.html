Who We Are

Bleenz Car Wash is a Houston-based mobile car wash and detailing service built around one idea: convenience without compromise. We believe your car deserves professional care, delivered right to your door — whether that's your home, office, or anywhere in between.

We connect Houston customers with skilled, vetted local detailing professionals who take pride in their work. No need to wait at a car wash. No need to rearrange your schedule. We come to you.

To make professional car detailing easy, affordable, and accessible for every Houston driver.
